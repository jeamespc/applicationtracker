How this works

Status key

InterestedNoted, not vetted/prioritized yet.
Ready to ApplyVetted, haven't sent anything yet.
AppliedSent, waiting to hear back.
In ProgressThey've responded, past the first stage. Good sign.
RejectedNot pursuing.

The colored square on each studio's row (and the dropdown next to it) shows/edits Status directly without opening the card. A square that's lit up with a glow means that studio has an active opening.

Openings check key

Open listingA specific, dated role was found on their site.
Open callStanding invite to apply speculatively (e.g. a dedicated internship email) — no posted vacancy.
Nothing postedSite was reachable, no opening or call found.
Couldn't determineSite was unreachable during the check — try again or check manually.

Each studio's card (expand it to see) has an Openings Check row showing which of the above applies, a link to what was checked, and the date.
